Our story

Made by hand, for hair with a mind of its own.

Neo Naturals began with a simple idea: natural hair deserves tools made specifically for it, not adapted from something else. Every comb and brush is shaped and finished by hand from solid wood, then engraved with our mark.

Alongside the wooden care line, we hand-carve loc accessories — picks, pins and tools shaped to work with locs, coils and curls through everyday life.
